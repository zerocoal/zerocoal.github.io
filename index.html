What does it cost to exit coal power?

Retiring 70% of the operating coal fleet now and cancelling every planned plant keeps coal power within the IEA's 1.5 °C sectoral budget. Change the assumptions below and watch the bill, and the savings, move.

How the explorer computes

Every bar is built from the paper's supplementary tables (Extended Data Tables 1–6). In the baseline, all figures match Figure 4 of the article.

  • Stranded assets: unrecovered construction cost of plants retired early (score below 0.626) plus cancelled planned plants. Fixed across scenarios.
  • Build costs: overnight cost of low-carbon capacity delivering the same electricity, at 2022 IEA costs and capacity factors.
  • Financing costs: interest at IRENA's 2024 solar PV WACC over the remaining coal lifetime, in present value. The +2 pp option uses the paper's sensitivity run.
  • Operating savings: avoided fuel, CO₂ and O&M costs over the coal plants' remaining lifetime, discounted at the WACC.
  • Approximations added by this tool: interpolation between carbon price scenarios; proportional scaling of financing with investment for alternative mixes and for partial concessional cover; lifetime variants added as net adjustments; storage applied to the World total only.

The analysis excludes grid expansion, demand growth, just-transition compensation and health co-benefits. It is a strictly financial view at IEA carbon prices.
